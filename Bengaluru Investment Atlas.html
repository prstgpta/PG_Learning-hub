About this data

Curated shortlist, not a live inventory feed. Prices are indicative advertised starting points for different unit sizes, not completed transactions. RERA references and possession dates come from linked listings unless noted as developer-published.

Pins were checked against OpenStreetMap on 28 Sep 2026: solid pins match a mapped project footprint, dashed pins are village or landmark centroids, faded pins mean the exact site is unpublished. Distances are straight-line; road distance and peak-hour travel time will be longer.

Established developers only (Prestige, Brigade, Godrej, Sobha, Sattva, Birla, Puravankara/Provident, Assetz, Century, Tata, Embassy; Bhartiya, TVS Emerald and Nambiar added on request). Entries tagged “Broker data” come from authorised-marketing-partner sites, not the developer, and are unverified; no official A-grade certification is implied. “KIADB” refers to the surrounding residential catchment, not a title designation. Price projections are scenario ranges from 99acres history, registration data and Anarock / PropTiger / Knight Frank reports (see Price outlook), not forecasts. Check land use on the KIADB portal and filings at Karnataka RERA.

How the projections are built

  • Corridor paths. Each corridor has an annual growth rate for years 1–3, 4–5, 6–7 and 8–10. The base case cools from the 2021–26 boom (13–19% a year on 99acres) towards Bengaluru’s long-run 7–9% nominal, then lifts where a funded metro or ring road opens (Blue Line ~2028, Red Line ~2033).
  • Bear repeats the 2019–21 lull (0–3% a year) for longer. Bull is roughly half the 2021–26 pace. The truth has usually sat nearer the base than either end over ten years.
  • Project adjustments (percentage points a year): plots +0.5 base (−1 bear, +1.5 bull) because land does not depreciate but is illiquid; resale over 10 years old −1.5; resale 5–10 years old −0.5; broker-only pre-launches without a RERA number −0.5 base and −1.5 bear for launch-price and delivery risk.
  • Values are nominal ₹ on the indicative entry price, before stamp duty, brokerage, GST, maintenance, capital-gains tax and ~4–5% inflation. Rent is not included here; use Compare & model for that.
  • Asking vs. registered. Rates are portal asking prices. Registered deal values are 20–45% lower in Bagalur, Sarjapur Road and Hoskote, so real resale exits may land below these lines.
  • Under-construction exits before OC usually need a developer assignment (transfer fee, lock-in). Plot and township phases can stall for years if a launch wave arrives nearby.
