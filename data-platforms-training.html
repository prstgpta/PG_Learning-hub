2 min

Why should I care?

Every company runs on data, and that data needs a home.

📥 Collect

Sales, customers, sensors, websites and apps produce data every second.

🗄️ Store

It has to live somewhere safe that can grow as the data grows.

🔍 Analyze

Turn raw numbers into reports, dashboards and answers.

🤖 Predict

Use AI to forecast, recommend and automate.

💡 Think of it like this: data is the raw ingredients. These platforms are the kitchens where the ingredients become meals (insights).
2 min

Four words to know

That's all the jargon you need today.

☁️ Cloud

Renting computers over the internet instead of buying your own. You pay only for what you use.

🏬 Data Warehouse

A neat, organized store of cleaned data, built for fast reports. Like a tidy library.

🌊 Data Lake

A huge, low-cost store for all data: messy files, images, logs. Like a big storage unit.

🏠 Lakehouse

A mix of the two: low-cost storage like a lake, organized like a warehouse.

The big picture: landlords vs. tenants

These four aren't the same kind of thing.

🏪 Databricks & SnowflakeSpecialized "shops" that rent space on a cloud and run there
🏙️ AWS & AzureThe "landlords": they own the land, buildings and electricity (the cloud), and also sell their own tools
💡 Databricks and Snowflake run on top of AWS or Azure (or Google Cloud). So companies often use both: for example, "Snowflake on AWS".
1.5 min Cloud provider

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

The biggest and oldest cloud: a huge hardware store.

🧰 Analogy: a giant hardware store with every tool you can imagine. You can build anything, but you have to put it together yourself.
  • Good at: huge choice, massive scale, strong security
  • Data tools: S3 (storage), Redshift (warehouse), SageMaker & Bedrock (AI)
  • Watch out: many separate pieces, so you need skilled engineers to connect them
  • Best for: companies already using AWS that have strong tech teams
1.5 min Cloud provider

Microsoft Azure

Microsoft's cloud, built to work with the tools you already use.

🏢 Analogy: a furnished office in the same building as your Outlook, Teams, Excel and Power BI. Everything is next door.
  • Good at: fitting smoothly with Microsoft 365, Power BI and Copilot
  • Data tools: Microsoft Fabric (all-in-one analytics), Azure OpenAI (AI)
  • Watch out: you become more tied to Microsoft; Fabric is still new
  • Best for: organizations that already run mostly on Microsoft
1.5 min Data & AI platform

Databricks

The "Lakehouse": a workshop for data builders and AI.

👩‍🍳 Analogy: a professional chef's kitchen. It handles any raw ingredient (messy data, images, text) and lets expert chefs cook anything, including AI.
  • Good at: processing large amounts of data, machine learning, AI
  • Works on: AWS, Azure and Google Cloud
  • Watch out: best suited to technical teams (data engineers, data scientists)
  • Best for: companies building AI or handling large, messy data
1.5 min Data platform

Snowflake

The "AI Data Cloud": simple, fast answers from organized data.

📚 Analogy: a well-run library with a helpful librarian. Ask a question and get an answer fast. Sharing a "book" with a partner is easy too.
  • Good at: ease of use, fast reports, sharing data with partners
  • Works on: AWS, Azure and Google Cloud
  • Watch out: costs can climb for very heavy processing jobs
  • Best for: business analysts, reporting and dashboards

Which one when?

A simple rule of thumb.

"We mainly need reports and dashboards."

➡️ Snowflake

"We want to build AI or crunch huge, messy data."

➡️ Databricks

"We live in Outlook, Teams and Power BI."

➡️ Azure (Microsoft Fabric)

"We're already on AWS with a strong tech team."

➡️ AWS

🤝 Remember: it's rarely "one or the other." Many companies use Databricks and/or Snowflake on AWS or Azure.

Quick quiz 🧠

Click a question to reveal the answer.

1. Which two are "cloud landlords"?

✅ AWS and Azure. They provide the underlying cloud that Databricks and Snowflake run on.

2. Your finance team just wants easy, fast reports. Which fits best?

✅ Snowflake: simple to use and great for reporting.

3. Your team wants to build an AI model from millions of customer photos. Which fits best?

✅ Databricks: built for large, messy data and AI.

4. True or false: you must pick only one of the four.

✅ False. They're often used together, for example "Databricks on Azure".

1 min

Key takeaways 🎯

  • ☁️ AWS & Azure are the landlords: they provide the cloud.
  • 👩‍🍳 Databricks is the chef's kitchen: best for AI and big, messy data.
  • 📚 Snowflake is the smart library: best for easy reports and sharing.
  • 🤝 They're often used together, not instead of each other.
  • 💰 All are pay-as-you-go. Switching things off saves money.
  • 🧭 The right choice depends on your people, your data and your goals.
